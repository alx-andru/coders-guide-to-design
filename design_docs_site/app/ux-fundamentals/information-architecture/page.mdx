### 6. Information Architecture (IA): Organizing Content

IA is about making information findable and understandable.

*   **Use Clear and Consistent Navigation Labels:** Ensure your navigation menus, button labels, and links use clear, concise, and predictable language that users will understand. Avoid jargon where possible.
*   **Think About Content Grouping:** Logically group related information and features together. This applies to navigation menus, settings pages, and how content is presented on a page.
*   **Consider User Flow:** How will users move from one piece of information or task to another? Aim for logical and efficient pathways.
*   **Prioritize Key Information:** Make the most important information or frequently used features easy to find. Don't bury critical functionality deep within menus.
